# Changelog

## v1.0.0 - 2026-04-08

Initial release of Kale Kronikleri (Castle Chronicles).

### Features

- **12 Tower Types** across 4 categories (Damage, Control, Defense, Magic), each with 4 upgrade tiers
  - Damage: Arrow, Fire, Lightning, Cannon
  - Control: Ice, Poison, Water
  - Defense: Spike Wall, Support
  - Magic: Wizard, Dark, Holy

- **Synergy System** with 11 tower combos
  - 7 dual-tower synergies (Shock Wave, Steam Damage, Freeze-Shatter, Acid Trap, Burning Acid, Balance Blast, Ice Prison)
  - 4 triple-tower synergies (Hell Line, Ice Age, Apocalypse, Wrath of the Gods)

- **12 Enemy Types** across 4 difficulty tiers
  - Easy: Soldier, Cavalry, Goblin
  - Medium: Armored Giant, Undead, Shield Bearer, Healer
  - Hard: Burrower, Troll, Dark Knight
  - Boss: Shadow Lord, Dragon Emperor

- **Wave System** with procedurally varied compositions, multi-path spawning (spread, focused, pincer patterns), and scaling difficulty

- **3 Spells**: Fire Rain, Ice Storm, Castle Repair

- **Procedural Map Generation** with quality validation (minimum path length, tower spot checks, edge-hugging prevention)

- **5 Difficulty Tiers** with unique biomes
  - Apprentice (Forest, 30 waves)
  - Knight (Desert, 40 waves)
  - Lord (Snow, 50 waves)
  - King (Volcano, 60 waves)
  - Legend (Darkness, 80 waves)

- **Meta Progression** with 4 skill trees and 28 unlockable abilities
  - War Tree: Combat power upgrades
  - Discovery Tree: Knowledge and exploration bonuses
  - Castle Tree: Defense, healing, and economy
  - Legend Tree: Endgame abilities gated by run count

- **Roguelike Buff System**: Choose from 8 different buffs every 5 waves

- **Weekly Mutation System**: 2 random game modifiers rotate each week (Fast Enemies, Tough Enemies, Boss Rush, etc.)

- **Daily Goals System**: 3 rotating daily objectives for Spirit rewards

- **Fully Offline**: No internet required, no ads, no in-app purchases
