## Full Description (max 4000 chars)

Kale Kronikleri (Castle Chronicles) is a tower defense game that combines deep strategy with roguelike mechanics. Strategically position 12 different tower types to defend your castle, discover powerful synergies, and defeat waves of enemies!

--- TOWERS ---

12 unique tower types across 4 categories:

Damage: Arrow Tower, Fire Tower, Lightning Tower, Cannon Tower
Control: Ice Tower, Poison Tower, Water Tower
Defense: Spike Wall, Support Tower
Magic: Wizard Tower, Dark Tower, Holy Tower

Each tower can be upgraded through 4 tiers. Transform the Arrow Tower into "Storm Rain", the Ice Tower into "Absolute Zero", or the Fire Tower into "Hellfire"!

--- SYNERGY SYSTEM ---

Place towers strategically next to each other to create powerful combos:

Water + Lightning = Shock Wave: Chain damage doubles on wet enemies!
Ice + Fire = Steam Damage: Area DoT + vision block
Ice + Arrow = Freeze-Shatter: 2x damage to frozen targets
Poison + Spike Wall = Acid Trap: Slow + armor melt
Dark + Holy = Balance Blast: Instant area nuke

Discover triple tower synergies too: Hell Line, Ice Age, Apocalypse, and more!

--- ENEMIES ---

12 different enemy types challenge your strategy:

From basic Soldiers, swift Cavalry, and cunning Goblin Thieves to Armored Giants, Shield Bearers, and Healers -- every enemy demands a different approach. Burrowers come from underground, Trolls regenerate health, Dark Knights pierce armor.

Every 10 waves the Shadow Lord boss appears, and every 30 waves the fearsome Dragon Emperor awaits!

--- SPELLS ---

Use powerful spells in critical moments:
Fire Rain: Area damage to all enemies
Ice Storm: Slow all enemies for 3 seconds
Castle Repair: Restore 10% of castle HP

--- PROCEDURAL MAPS ---

Every game is different! The procedural map generator creates a unique battlefield each run. Multiple enemy paths, strategic tower placement spots, and difficulty-specific biomes: Forest, Desert, Snow, Volcano, and Darkness!

--- ROGUELIKE ELEMENTS ---

Every 5 waves, choose from powerful buff options: Damage Boost, Rapid Fire, Extended Range, Gold Hunter, and more. Every run offers a unique experience.

Weekly mutations keep the game fresh: Fast Enemies, Armored Army, Boss Rush and other modifiers change every week.

--- META PROGRESSION ---

Earn permanent upgrades across 4 skill trees:

War Tree: Tower power and combat bonuses
Discovery Tree: Knowledge advantage and exploration bonuses
Castle Tree: Defense, healing, and economy
Legend Tree: Powerful abilities (requires many runs)

Unlock 28 different abilities by collecting Spirit points! Gain 5 seconds of invincibility when HP drops to 1 with "Last Breath", earn bonus gold each wave with "Treasure Rooms", or unlock endless mode with "Eternal Castle"!

--- DAILY GOALS ---

Earn Spirit with new goals every day: "Kill 200 enemies", "3 perfect waves", "Form 2 synergies", and more.

--- DIFFICULTY LEVELS ---

5 difficulty levels:
Apprentice (30 waves) - Lord (50 waves) - Legend (80 waves)
As you progress, face new biomes and tougher enemies.

Fully playable offline. No ads. No in-app purchases.
